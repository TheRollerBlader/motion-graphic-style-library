# Style catalog

Pick one style for the whole video. Do not mix two.

Match the user's ask against "Use it for". If two styles fit equally well, ask which one before drawing. If they named a style, use that one even if another also fits.

| Style | Library | Use it for | How it moves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `swiss` Editorial Swiss | `motionstyle_swiss` | strategy explainers, essays, product positioning, serious software | Mechanical and exact. Elements wipe, crop, and settle their tracking. They arrive and stop. Nothing bounces, blurs, or overshoots. |
| `kinetic` Kinetic Type | `motionstyle_kinetic` | hooks, title cards, manifesto lines, social openers | Heavy and fast. Words scale down into place and land with a short overshoot. The underline finishes after the word, not before it. |
| `glass` Soft Glass | `motionstyle_glass` | AI products, fintech, calm explainers, feature tours | Float, don't snap. Panels rise a short distance and fade. Nothing overshoots. Blur on the backdrop stays constant. Do not animate the blur radius. |
| `brutalist` Neo-Brutalist | `motionstyle_brutalist` | teaching explainers, product how-tos, opinionated software, classroom-to-camera lessons | Physical and springy. Cards pop into their shadow. The shadow grows with the card, it is not a fade. Held cards rock by a fraction of a degree. |
| `chalk` Chalk Classroom | `motionstyle_chalk` | lessons, how-it-works, tutorials, classroom explainers | Draw-on. Strokes reveal along their length, the way chalk leaves the stick. Words fade in only after the drawing they name has started. Dust settles. Nothing slams. |
| `isometric` Isometric Studio | `motionstyle_isometric` | systems, architecture, pipelines, product maps | Blocks drop onto the ground plane and settle. Later blocks wait their stagger. Nothing spins in true 3D. The camera is locked to the isometric angle. |
| `signal` Data Signal | `motionstyle_signal` | metrics, research findings, earnings explainers, product analytics | Instrument-like. Bars grow from the baseline, lines draw left to right, and numerals count. Easing arrives and stops. No bounce. |
| `papercut` Paper Cut | `motionstyle_papercut` | stories, consumer brands, process steps, gentle explainers | Sheets travel a short distance into the stack, back layer first. The top sheet arrives last. Motion eases out. The stack then breathes one or two pixels. |
| `neon` Neon Night | `motionstyle_neon` | launches, games, music, nightlife products | A tube flickers on, then the glow breathes. The flicker is short and happens once. The pulse is the idle, not another flicker. |
| `documentary` Warm Documentary | `motionstyle_documentary` | origin stories, interviews, narrative explainers, field notes | Slow. Pictures drift with a Ken Burns scale. Type fades. The lower third slides a short distance and stays. Nothing bounces, slams, or draws on like a diagram. |
| `playful` Playful Flat | `motionstyle_playful` | onboarding, consumer apps, friendly explainers, first-run tours | Squash and stretch, then a small overshoot. Elements hop into place one after another. The idle is a bob, not a spin. |
| `blueprint` Blueprint | `motionstyle_blueprint` | engineering, how it's built, process, technical explainers | A draftsman's hand. The outline draws, ticks appear when the line reaches them, then the label fades. No bounce and no fill animation. |
| `gradient` Liquid Gradient | `motionstyle_gradient` | brand films, premium product, wellness, launch titles | Ambient and slow. Blobs drift on long loops. Type rises a few pixels and fades. Nothing snaps, slams, or draws on. |
| `comic` Comic Pop | `motionstyle_comic` | social explainers, contrasts, punchy education, wrong versus right | A hit. The burst scales in with a short overshoot, the bubble follows, and a two-step shake sells the impact. Then it holds. Do not keep shaking. |

After choosing, read that style's skill (`style-<id>/SKILL.md`) and follow it literally.
